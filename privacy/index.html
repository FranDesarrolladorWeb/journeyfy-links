Journeyfy Privacy Policy

Last updated: 29 August 2026

Journeyfy is a collaborative travel-planning app for iOS. This policy explains what data the app handles and how. The short version: your data stays in Apple's iCloud (CloudKit), we run no servers of our own, we show no ads, and we never sell or share your data with anyone.

What we collect and why

  • Name and email — provided by Sign in with Apple, used to identify you to your travel group (your display name on messages, expenses and polls).
  • Content you create — trips, chat messages, expenses, polls, checklists, itinerary items, and the photos/videos you choose to share with a trip group.
  • Approximate location — only while you use the trip map, to center it near you. It is never stored or shared.

Where it lives

All data is stored in Apple's CloudKit service, tied to your iCloud account and protected by Apple's security infrastructure. We operate no servers of our own and have no analytics or tracking SDKs. Trip content is visible only to the members of that trip.

What we don't do

  • No advertising, no trackers, no analytics SDKs.
  • No selling, renting or sharing of your data with third parties.
  • No collection of data beyond what the features above need.

Content safety

You can report any message and block any user directly in the app. Reported content is hidden for you immediately and reviewed by us within 24 hours; accounts that post objectionable content are removed. There is zero tolerance for objectionable content or abusive behavior.

Deleting your data

In the app, go to Settings → Delete Account. This permanently deletes your profile (name and photo) and removes you from all trips. You can also delete individual messages and photos you created at any time.
