Help

  • Contact us: frandeveloper4@gmail.com
  • Forgot your password? On the email sign-in screen, choose to reset it: we email you a 6-digit code to type in the app with your new password.
  • An invite link doesn't open the app? Install Journeyfy, then tap the link again. On Android, tap Open in Journeyfy on the page the link opens.
  • Something offensive in a chat or a public trip? Report it from the app, or write to us. We review reports within 24 hours.
  • Delete your account: in the app, Settings → Delete Account, or without the app.
